Africa does not lack resources.

We have not yet unlocked their full value.

The future is not simply renewable. It is circular.

Africa produces enormous quantities of agricultural residues, organic waste, municipal waste, used lubricants and difficult plastic waste — much of it underused, poorly managed, dumped or burned. CircularEnergies exists to change that equation.

Where others see disposal costs, we see feedstock. Where others see residues, we see energy. Where others see waste oil, we see recoverable resources. Where others see plastic pollution, we see an opportunity for recovery.

We are building an economy where resources circulate, value stays closer to home, and waste becomes part of the solution.

Our solutions

Six pathways, one circular logic.

Each product line starts with a resource stream most treat as waste, and ends with an energy, fuel or industrial output built around Africa's own feedstocks.

Bio-CNG & Biogas

Waste into clean, dependable energy.

Feedstock: Suitable organic waste and biomass

Pathway: Anaerobic digestion; biomethane upgraded and compressed where technically and economically appropriate

Applications: Industrial boilers, commercial kitchens, transport, agro-processing, captive power

Learn more about Bio-CNG

Cassava Bioethanol

From African agriculture to cleaner fuel.

Feedstock: Cassava and other suitable agricultural resources

Pathway: Farmer → aggregation → processing → ethanol

Applications: Fuel blending, industrial and chemical feedstock, clean cooking

Learn more about Bioethanol

Biomass Briquettes

Agricultural residues into useful solid fuel.

Feedstock: Rice husks, sawdust, groundnut shells, corn residues

Pathway: Collection, drying, densification

Applications: Commercial kitchens, agro-processing, institutional and industrial thermal use

Learn more about Briquettes

Sustainable Aviation Fuel

Building pathways toward lower-carbon African aviation.

Feedstock: Sustainable feedstocks, subject to availability

Pathway: Advanced processing, dependent on technology, certification and offtake

Status: Developing Africa's next generation of SAF pathways — not yet in commercial production

Discuss SAF development

Plastic Pyrolysis Oil

Giving difficult plastic waste another life.

Feedstock: Sorted, difficult-to-recycle waste plastics

Pathway: Sorting → processing → pyrolysis → recovered oil

Applications: Industrial uses dependent on quality, contaminants and specification

Learn more about Pyrolysis

Re-refined Waste Oil

Renewing what we already have.

Feedstock: Used lubricating engine oil

Pathway: Collection → treatment → re-refining → recovered base oil

Note: Recovered base oils are distinct from finished lubricants

Learn more about Re-refining
