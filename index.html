The Challenge

Nigerian operators are stuck in an energy cost spiral with no clear exit.

Fuel subsidy removal, naira devaluation, and rising diesel prices have made energy the single biggest cost pressure on Nigerian SMEs — yet most operators have no access to credible, affordable analysis of the alternatives.

PROBLEM 01
⛽

Petrol & Diesel Costs Are Unsustainable

Since subsidy removal, pump prices have tripled. Fleet operators, manufacturers, and hospitality businesses are watching margins collapse — but switching fuels without rigorous analysis is a risk most can't afford to take blind.

PROBLEM 02
📉

FX Exposure Compounds Every Devaluation

Nigeria imports most of its refined fuel. Every naira slide directly raises your operating cost. Domestic alternatives — CNG, LPG, biogas — break this dependency, but qualifying your operation for them requires specialist analysis most operators can't access locally.

PROBLEM 03
🔍

No Affordable, Rigorous Analysis Available

International consultants charge in dollars and take months. Local engineers don't cover the financial modelling. Banks won't finance a transition without a feasibility study. CircularEnergies was built to fill exactly this gap — at a price Nigerian SMEs can actually afford.
